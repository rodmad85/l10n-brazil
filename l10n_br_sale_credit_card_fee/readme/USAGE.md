To use this module, you need to:

1. Configure the fee tables in *Sales → Configuration → Credit Card
   Administrators*.
2. On a quotation or sale order, go to the *Invoice Plan* tab and click
   *⇒ Create Invoice Plan*.
3. In the wizard, choose the number of installments and the **Card
   Administrator**. The wizard shows the applicable **Fee (%)**, the
   fee amount and the order total including the fee (**Amount + Fee**).
4. Enable **Sum Fee** to add the credit card fee to the order total and
   to include it proportionally in each installment invoice. Leave it
   unchecked to track the fee without charging it.
5. Confirm the wizard and invoice the installments from the invoice
   plan; each customer invoice will contain a *Credit Card Fee* line
   with its proportional share of the fee.
